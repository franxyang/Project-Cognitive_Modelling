# What it means

`Intrinsic load` comes from the structure of the material and the learner's current knowledge.
`Extraneous load` comes from the way the material is presented or organized.

# Why it matters for my project

This distinction is the backbone of the model.
It supports separating structure-driven cost from design-induced cost.

# What it is often confused with

- difficulty in general
- amount of information alone
- germane processing as a separate load source
