# What it means

`Segmentation` is the external division of learning material into parts.
`Chunking` is the internal grouping or compression of multiple elements into a more manageable unit.

# Why it matters for my project

Your project is mainly about segmentation, but it sits close to chunking and compression models.
The distinction prevents the model from blurring instructional design with internal representation.

# What it is often confused with

- any kind of simplification
- summarization
- schema formation in general
