# What it means

`Task complexity` is about the structure and interaction of information that must be coordinated.
`Task difficulty` is about how hard the task is for a learner in practice.

# Why it matters for my project

Your model is about structural cost, so it needs complexity rather than a vague notion of difficulty.
This distinction helps justify why `w_uv` and `q_j` belong in the model.

# What it is often confused with

- sheer amount of material
- subjective effort alone
- poor performance for any reason
